# Identity Development

Identity emerges through interactions among biological predispositions, developmental experiences, social environments, self-concept construction, and contextual activation.

## Developmental Flow

Distal Influences -> Proximal Influences -> Self -> Self-Concept -> Identity Formation -> Active Identity -> Behavior -> Feedback -> Self-Concept Revision

## Distal Influences
- Genetics
- Temperament
- Neurobiology
- Early Development

## Proximal Influences
- Family
- Attachment
- Peers
- Education
- Culture
- Institutions
- Life Events

## Identity is Dynamic
Identity is contextually activated and continuously updated through feedback and experience.
