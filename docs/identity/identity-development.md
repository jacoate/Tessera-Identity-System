# Identity Development

Version: 0.2 Provisional
Status: Expansion Scaffold / Source-Review Needed
Scope: identity development, self-concept, and future holistic model integration

---

## Purpose

This file sketches a provisional identity-development flow for Tessera's broader holistic model.

It is not currently a validated assessment model. It should be reviewed against empirical literature on self, self-concept, identity development, social identity, role identity, narrative identity, and contextual identity activation before being operationalized as official testing infrastructure.

---

## Developmental Flow

```text
Distal Influences
-> Proximal Influences
-> Self
-> Self-Concept
-> Identity Formation
-> Active Identity
-> Behavior
-> Feedback
-> Self-Concept Revision
```

---

## Identity is Dynamic

Identity may be contextually activated and continuously updated through feedback and experience.

This statement should be treated as a working model summary, not as a complete theory of identity.

---

## Relationship to Personality and Karma

Personality taxonomy describes tendencies and expression patterns.

Karma reviews moral-context and ethical-conflict patterns.

Identity development may provide context for why particular tendencies, values, roles, or interpretations become salient in specific situations.

These systems should remain distinct until their interactions are explicitly modeled and reviewed.
