# Three-Pass Assessment Methodology

## Purpose
Increase reliability while preserving meaningful variance.

## Pass One
Initial assessment.

## Pass Two
Alternative item arrangements and wording.

## Pass Three
Independent reassessment.

## Interpretation
Trait scores are evaluated for:
- Strength
- Consistency
- Variability

Variance is preserved as data and may indicate contextual expression, identity conflict, role compartmentalization, or developmental instability.
