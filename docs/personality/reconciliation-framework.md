# Reconciliation Framework

## Purpose
Reconciliation explains discrepancies between assessment outcomes, self-concept, observer reports, and behavioral evidence.

## Principle
Contradictions are treated as informative data rather than simple error.

## Reconciliation Categories
- Situational Restriction
- Social Inhibition
- Selective Expression
- Role Suppression
- Opportunity Deficit
- Self-Concept Distortion
- Adaptive Masking
- Measurement Error

## Outcome
Reconciliation supports identity calibration and self-understanding.
