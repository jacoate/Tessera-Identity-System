# Proximal Influences

Version: 0.2 Provisional
Status: Expansion Scaffold / Source-Review Needed
Scope: future ecological, relational, and identity-development integration

---

## Purpose

Proximal influences are near-term environmental, relational, cultural, and institutional factors that may shape self-concept, identity development, personality expression, and moral-context interpretation.

This file is an expansion scaffold. It should not be treated as a validated Tessera module until source review and implementation design specify how these influences are represented.

---

## Categories

- Family Systems
- Attachment Relationships
- Peer Networks
- Education
- Community
- Institutions
- Cultural Participation
- Significant Life Events

---

## Working Principle

Proximal influences may continuously interact with active identity construction.

They can provide context for expression, inhibition, role suppression, values, belonging, conflict, and self-concept revision.

They should not be used to reduce behavior to a single environmental cause.

---

## Governance Boundary

Empirically backed ecological, developmental, cultural, and relational information should be preserved and cited during future expansion.

Speculative Tessera mappings should be labeled as provisional and should not overwrite established science.

---

## Digital Expansion Note

Future digital infrastructure may represent proximal influences as contextual background variables, role/context tags, developmental prompts, or non-scoring interpretation context.

They should not be treated as direct personality or Karma scores unless future validation supports such use.
