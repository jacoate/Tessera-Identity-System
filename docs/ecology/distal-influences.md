# Distal Influences

Version: 0.2 Provisional
Status: Expansion Scaffold / Source-Review Needed

## Purpose

Distal influences are broad developmental conditions that may shape later identity construction, personality expression, and moral-context development.

This file is an expansion scaffold. It should not be treated as a validated Tessera module until the relevant empirical literature is reviewed and the model specifies how these influences are represented in cards, interpretation, or future digital infrastructure.

## Working Principle

Distal influences may establish constraints, probabilities, vulnerabilities, and opportunities that interact with proximal environments across development.

They should not be used to deterministically explain behavior or moral character.

---

## Governance Boundary

Empirically backed developmental, biological, and ecological information should be preserved and cited during future expansion.

Speculative Tessera mappings should be labeled as provisional and should not overwrite established science.

---

## Digital Expansion Note

Future digital infrastructure may represent distal influences as contextual background variables, source-lineage fields, developmental-history prompts, or non-scoring interpretation context.

They should not be treated as direct personality scores unless future validation supports such use.
